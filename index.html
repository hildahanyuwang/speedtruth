Is the posted speed limit right for this road?

Most speed work asks whether drivers obey the sign. This scores 14,711 road segments in Thailand and Maharashtra against the speed a person outside the car can survive, checks the number against the law that set it, and names the office that can lawfully change it.

The result in four numbers

14,711segments scored against a Safe-System survivable speed, of a 69,966-segment network
4,861operate at least 30 km/h above a survivable speed (Critical, after imagery correction)
4,017fast segments beside a school or market
1,736re-banded by street imagery — and the net movement was downward

On the priority corridors drivers often travel below the limit, so the finding is not that people speed. It is that the limit itself was set for a road that no longer exists: a bypass posted at highway speed that informal traffic and roadside development have since grown into.

What makes it checkable

  • The model audits itself. Twelve documented places where the first design was wrong and was rebuilt, including a factor removed for correlating at 0.80 with another.
  • It takes alarms away. A vision model reads road condition from Mapillary imagery and feeds it back into the score. It moved 1,736 segments and mostly found the rule too harsh, not new hidden danger — 1,552 down, 184 up.
  • Coverage is stated, not implied. Imagery verifies 3,537 segments: 24% of the segments scored, about 5% of the full network. Everything outside that is screened, not verified.
  • The weak half is named. Of 1,473 Priority 1 segments, only 481 hold under nearly any weighting; the other 992 depend on the weighting chosen here.
  • Anyone can recompute it. python reproduce.py checks 44 of the 61 published figures with no credentials and no confidential data, and names the other 17 rather than dropping them.
  • Honest about the invisible fleet. Auto-rickshaws, modified and human-powered vehicles do not appear in probe data and barely appear in OpenStreetMap, so scores on those corridors are a conservative lower bound.
